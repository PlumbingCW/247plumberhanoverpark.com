Privacy Policy

What we collect when you contact us, and what we do with it.

Information we collect

When you call or email us, we collect the details you give us, such as your name, phone number, service address and a description of the plumbing problem. This website does not have forms or accounts.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

How we use information

We use your details to dispatch a plumber, give you a price, schedule work and follow up on the job. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set advertising cookies. Fonts load from Google Fonts and the map embeds load from Google Maps, which may receive standard browser information such as your IP address under their own policies.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing contact@247plumberhanoverpark.com.
