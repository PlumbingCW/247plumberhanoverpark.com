Sewage Backing Up? Do This Now

Stop using water at once. A running faucet or flush adds to what is already coming up. Keep children and pets out of the area, because sewage carries bacteria.

  • Do not run the washer, dishwasher or showers.
  • Do not mix or pour chemicals into the drain.
  • Lift valuables and boxes off the basement floor.
  • Open a window if the smell is strong, but do not touch standing water.

Call us and say how many drains are affected. We answer around the clock, and a main line backup is treated as an emergency. If you smell gas rather than sewage, leave the house and call the gas company.

What Sewer Line Cleaning Is

Your building sewer is the pipe that runs from the house to the village main. It is typically four to six inches across and carries everything the house drains. Cleaning means we run a heavy cable or jetter through it and remove what is blocking it.

Most main line stoppages come from tree roots, grease, paper, wipes, or a broken section that traps debris. We can usually tell the cause from the cable and from what comes out. A camera then confirms it.

Why Older Hanover Park Sewers Clog

The bulk of local homes were built in the 1960s and early 1970s. Building sewers from that era are often clay tile or cast iron. Clay comes in short sections with joints every few feet, and each joint is a place for roots to enter.

Cast iron rusts and sheds scale. Over decades, the bore narrows and a rough surface grabs paper. For a homeowner, this explains why a house may need its main cleaned more often than a newer neighbor.

Hanover Park has more than 200 miles of storm and sanitary sewers. Your building sewer connects to the village main at the property line. The village is responsible for the main, and you are responsible for your own line, so a camera helps decide which side has the problem.

How We Clean a Main Sewer Line

Locate the cleanout

We look for a cleanout in the yard, basement or crawl area. Village repair rules call for a cleanout at grade, so newer repairs usually have one.

Set up and protect the area

We lay down protection, cap off nearby drains if needed, and make sure no sewage can reach finished spaces.

Cable the line

A drum or sectional machine with a cutting head clears the stoppage. For roots we use a bladed head, and for grease a chain or spinning head.

Flush and test

We run water and watch it move. A line that drains fast and holds is a good sign.

Camera the pipe when it makes sense

A recorded look shows whether the cleaning worked and what the pipe needs, as covered in sewer inspection.

Cable or Jet, and Which Is Right

A cable cuts through the stoppage and gets service back quickly. Jetting washes the wall and reduces the chance of a repeat. For a line that backs up often, our hydro jetting work is the better long-term fix.

If the camera shows damage, cleaning is only a stopgap. A root-filled joint will keep admitting roots until the joint is replaced or lined. Village rules state relining requires descaling and a recorded camera inspection and is not allowed on defective pipe.

Flood-prone parts of town

Village hazard planning lists the Highlands subdivision, Schick Road, Anne Fox School Creek and the Barrington and Irving Park Road area as flood-prone. If you live nearby, a backup during heavy rain may be caused by stormwater entering the sewer, not a clog. Tell us if it only happens in storms.

What Affects the Cost of Sewer Line Cleaning

The price depends on the blockage and the access, so we explain it after seeing the cleanout and hearing the symptoms. These are the main factors.

Cleanout access

A clean, reachable cleanout speeds the job. If none exists, we may have to work through a toilet or roof vent.

What is blocking the line

Paper is quick. Root masses and grease need heavier heads and several passes.

Length and size of the line

A longer run to the main takes longer to clear and to flush.

Pipe condition

Offset joints and cracked pipe make cleaning slower and riskier.

Camera work

Recording the pipe before and after adds time, but it documents the line.

Codes and standards that apply

Cleaning a building sewer is separate from repairing one. Under the village rules for sanitary sewer repair, any repair work needs a recorded inspection, a JULIE dig number (800-892-0123) and a licensed plumber when it is within 5 feet of the building.
