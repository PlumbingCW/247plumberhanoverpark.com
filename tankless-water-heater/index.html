How a tankless water heater works

A tankless heater has no storage tank. When you open a hot tap, a flow sensor tells the unit to fire a burner or energize elements, and water heats as it passes through a heat exchanger. The hot water does not run out the way a tank can, but the unit has a maximum flow rate.

That flow limit is the key to sizing. If two showers and a dishwasher run at once, a unit sized for one shower will deliver lukewarm water. We size the unit by adding up the fixtures you use at the same time and the temperature rise your incoming water needs in winter.

Most whole-house tankless units sold today are gas, rated around 199,000 BTU per hour at the top end. That is much more gas than a typical tank heater burns, so the existing gas line and meter capacity matter. Electric whole-house units can draw enough amperage to need a service panel upgrade.

What a conversion from a tank involves

Check gas and electric capacity

We total your appliance gas loads and measure the pipe run to see whether the line can feed a high-BTU unit. Electric models get a panel review.

Plan venting

Condensing units use PVC or polypropylene vent and exit through a wall. Non-condensing units need stainless vent. The old chimney is not reused as is.

Plan water and condensate

We add isolation valves with service ports so the unit can be flushed. Condensing models need a drain and sometimes a neutralizer.

Mount and connect

The unit hangs on a wall, so it frees floor space. We connect water, gas, vent, and power, then purge air from the lines.

Set and test

We set the temperature, verify flow, and check for error codes. We then show you the controls.

Hard water and tankless maintenance

Tankless heat exchangers have narrow passages, and scale from hard water can block them. Hanover Park receives Lake Michigan water via the Chicago system. The Streamwood 2023 report for the same supply lists 8 grains per gallon, which is moderately hard. That is enough that tankless units here usually need regular flushing.

Manufacturers generally call for an annual flush with a descaling solution, often white vinegar or a labeled descaler, circulated through the heat exchanger using the service valves. Skipping it leads to error codes, reduced flow, and a lukewarm output. A water softener reduces scale at the source and extends the time between flushes.

Common tankless error causes

  • Ignition failure from a closed gas valve, low gas pressure, or a dirty flame rod.
  • Overheating shutdowns from scale on the heat exchanger.
  • Flow errors from clogged inlet screens or a stuck flow sensor.
  • Condensate drain blockages on condensing models.
  • Vent pressure faults from blocked or crushed exhaust piping.

Hanover Park homes and tankless readiness

Many homes here date from the 1960s and 1970s, so gas lines are often sized for a furnace and a tank heater rather than a 199,000 BTU unit. That does not rule tankless out, but it can mean a larger branch line. We measure first so you do not buy a unit your gas supply cannot feed.

Venting is easier than with an old chimney because condensing models exit through a side wall. Basements with low ceilings are fine, since the unit mounts on the wall. Permits come through the village Inspectional Services Division, and registered contractors only.

What affects tankless water heater cost

Tankless jobs vary more than tank swaps. These are the factors that shape the total.

Unit capacity and brand

Higher flow and condensing efficiency raise the unit price.

Gas line and meter work

A larger gas branch line is the most common extra.

Venting route

Longer or more complex vent runs through walls and joists add labor.

Water treatment and valves

Service valves, a condensate neutralizer, and optional softening add parts.

Permit and electrical

Permit fees and a new outlet or circuit for the unit may apply.

Codes and standards that apply

Tankless installs follow the manufacturer's venting and gas sizing instructions together with the Illinois Plumbing Code (77 Ill. Adm. Code 890). Check that the installer is registered with the village before any permit is pulled.
