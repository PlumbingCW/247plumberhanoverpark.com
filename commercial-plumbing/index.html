Commercial plumbing emergency: what to do right now

Find the main shut-off for the building and close it if water is spreading. Keep staff and customers away from wet floors. If sewage is backing up through floor drains, stop using restrooms and sinks and call.

Photograph the damage before cleanup. Note the time you noticed it. That helps with insurance and gives us a clearer picture for the repair.

Gas smell with water heater trouble?

Leave the area and call your gas utility from outside. Do not turn switches on or off. Plumbing work comes after the building is safe.

Grease traps and kitchen drains

Restaurants, bakeries and cafes send fats, oils and grease down the drain every day. A grease trap or interceptor catches it before it reaches the sewer. When the trap is full, grease passes through and hardens in the lateral line.

Many municipalities follow a 25 percent rule: clean the interceptor when grease and solids reach a quarter of its liquid depth. Check your lease and local requirements, and keep pumping records. Frequency depends on how busy the kitchen is.

Backflow prevention

Backflow devices keep dirty water from flowing back into the drinking supply. Commercial buildings with irrigation, boilers, fire sprinklers or process equipment usually need them. Backflow assemblies generally need periodic testing. Streamwood, next door, runs a cross-connection program through its Public Works department, and Hanover Park businesses should expect similar attention to this issue.

Codes, permits and registration

Illinois plumbing work follows the Illinois Plumbing Code, 77 Ill. Adm. Code 890. Plumbers must be licensed by the Illinois Department of Public Health, and contractors must be registered with Hanover Park before permits are issued. Permits go through the Inspectional Services Division, which is closed on Wednesdays.

Commercial jobs often need more paperwork than residential ones, so plan ahead for remodels. Emergency repairs can usually start while permits are being sorted, but the permit still has to be filed.
