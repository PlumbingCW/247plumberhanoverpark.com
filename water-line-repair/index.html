What to do right now

If water is pooling in the yard or coming up through the pavement, close the main shutoff inside the house. That stops flow from the service line to your fixtures, though water can still escape outside until the line is isolated at the curb. Tell us what you see, and we will advise on the next step.

If the water is in the street or parkway and the break looks like it is on the main, call the village Public Works department so they can shut down the public side. Do not dig around the leak yourself. Buried gas, electric and communications lines share many of the same trenches.

What the water service line is

The water service line is the underground pipe that brings drinking water from the village main to your meter and into your home. In Hanover Park the main belongs to the village. The line from the curb stop to the house is generally the homeowner's responsibility, and that is the part we repair.

These lines have been in the ground since the home was built. Copper, galvanized steel, plastic and, in some older areas, lead may all be present. Each material fails differently, so we identify it before we choose a fix.

How we repair a buried water line

Locate the line

We trace the route using the meter location, the entry point and, if needed, acoustic or electronic locating gear.

Call JULIE

Before any digging, we request a locate through JULIE so underground utilities are marked. It is a legal step and a safety one.

Choose the method

A spot repair means excavating one small hole and replacing a damaged section. A full replacement may use an open trench or a trenchless method where the soil allows.

Replace the damaged pipe

We install new pipe suited to the supply, with proper fittings at the meter and entry point.

Test and restore

We pressurize the line, check for leaks, then backfill and tidy the area.

If you are not sure the leak is in the buried line at all, start with leak detection. It confirms the location before anyone digs. If the line is failing along its whole length, ask about repiping the inside run as well, since a new service line feeding old galvanized pipe wastes some of the benefit.

Lead service lines and Illinois law

Illinois passed the Lead Service Line Replacement and Notification Act, effective January 1, 2022. The law requires community water suppliers to inventory service line materials and plan for replacing lead lines. It also says that when a lead service line is repaired or upgraded, the entire lead line must be replaced, not just a portion.

Hanover Park keeps a service line inventory dashboard on its water quality page. Look up your address before you hire anyone. If your line is lead or unknown, tell us up front, because a repair on that line can change the scope from a small patch to a full replacement.

Water line repair in Hanover Park homes

Hanover Park grew fast in the 1960s, with Greenbrook first and Woodlake Trails and Olde Salem in the early 1970s. Service lines laid then are now fifty years old or more. Galvanized lines in particular can fill with rust and scale, which shows up as weak pressure and brown water long before a full break.

The village runs about 122 miles of water mains and serves around 10,826 metered customers. All of it is Lake Michigan water, supplied through the Northwest Suburban Municipal Joint Action Water Agency. Reports for the same Chicago supply describe moderately hard water, so scale buildup inside older iron lines is common. See our water softener installation page if scale is a concern at your tap.

Ground, frost and flood in the village

Frost can heave soil and stress a shallow line, and Hanover Park has seen cold snaps with single-digit lows, including the December 2010 winter storm. If the line was installed too shallow or runs under a driveway, shifting ground adds strain. After a deep freeze, watch your pressure.

Flood-prone areas listed in the Cook County hazard mitigation plan include the Highlands subdivision, Schick Road, the Barrington and Irving Park Road corridors, and the Anne Fox School Creek area. Saturated soil moves around buried pipe, so after a major flood, check pressure and your meter. If you see a sudden drop, call us.

What affects the cost of water line repair

Prices for service line work vary widely. These are the main factors.

Length and depth

A short spot repair is much smaller than replacing the line from the curb to the foundation.

Pipe material found

Lead, galvanized and copper lines each have different replacement rules and parts.

What sits over the line

Driveways, sidewalks, trees and landscaping add restoration work after the dig.

Excavation or trenchless

Open trenching costs differently than pipe bursting or pulling new pipe through the old path.

Permits and inspections

Water service work usually needs a village permit, and some steps need an inspection.

Insurance coverage for buried lines varies by policy and by cause, so ask your carrier whether a service line endorsement applies to your home.

Call and describe what you see. We will explain the likely steps and the factors that set the price before work begins.

For public side work in the right-of-way, the village Engineering department may need to approve or inspect. We coordinate that so you are not left waiting on paperwork.

Local note

Who is allowed to do this work in Hanover Park

Plumbing contractors working in Hanover Park must be registered with the village through the Inspectional Services Division. Registration requires IDPH 055 contractor registration and a 058 plumber licence, plus a Letter of Intent, and permits are not issued until registration is done. The licensing year runs February 1 to January 31.

That matters for you because an unregistered contractor cannot pull the permit for your water line job. Ask for proof, or check a licence on the IDPH online verification tool before you sign.

Source type: Hanover Park Inspectional Services and Illinois IDPH

Codes and standards that apply

A water service repair is governed by the Illinois Plumbing Code (77 Ill. Adm. Code 890), and work in the public right-of-way is approved and inspected by village Engineering. Call before digging, and use JULIE at 800-892-0123 for a dig number.
