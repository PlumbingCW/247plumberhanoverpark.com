About Hanover Park 24/7 Plumbing

A local plumbing company built around one promise: when something fails at 2 a.m., a person answers.

Call (630) 822-9290

Who we are

Hanover Park 24/7 Plumbing is based at 1645 Irving Park Rd in Hanover Park, Illinois. We handle emergency repairs and planned plumbing work for homes and small businesses in the village and five nearby towns.

The company is built around the emergency line. Calls are answered at any hour, and the dispatcher sends a licensed plumber to the address.

What we handle

  • Clogged drains, sewer backups and hydro jetting
  • Water heater repair, replacement and installation
  • Leak detection and burst pipe repair
  • Toilets, faucets and garbage disposals
  • Sump pumps and basement water problems

How we price and schedule

You get a clear price before work starts. Same-day appointments are offered when the schedule allows, and emergencies go to the front of the line.

How an emergency call works

A person answers and asks three things: your address, what is happening, and whether water is near anything electrical. Those answers decide how the job is dispatched and what we tell you to do before we arrive.

On arrival the plumber finds the cause, explains the options in plain words and gives you a price before any work begins. If the repair needs a permit, the plumber says so up front, because the village will not issue one until the contractor is registered.

Why local matters in Hanover Park

Hanover Park grew quickly after its 1958 incorporation, and many homes date from the 1960s and early 1970s. Plumbing that old tends to show the same failures: corroded supply pipe, slow sewer lines and water heaters on their second or third unit. Knowing that pattern helps a plumber diagnose a problem faster, because the likely causes are already narrowed down.

The water also matters. The village buys Lake Michigan water through the Northwest Suburban Municipal Joint Action Water Agency, and reports for that same Chicago supply describe moderately hard water. Scale builds up inside water heaters and on fixtures, so we flag it when it shows up in a repair.

Homes, apartments and small businesses

Most calls come from single-family homes and townhomes. We also work in apartment buildings, offices, restaurants and small retail spaces, where a stopped drain or a failed water heater costs the business money every hour it stays down. See commercial plumbing for how that work differs.

What we ask of you

A little preparation makes any visit faster. Know where your main water shutoff is, keep the area around the problem clear, and tell us about any earlier repairs you remember. If the problem is a leak, photographs taken before cleanup help with insurance claims.

If a repair needs access to a wall, ceiling or crawl space, the plumber explains what will be opened and what is left for you to patch. Nothing gets cut without a conversation first.

After the job, you get a plain explanation of what failed and what to watch for next. For aging pipe or a heater near the end of its life, we say so honestly and let you decide on timing. Our guides on water heater replacement and repiping explain how those decisions usually play out.

We also keep the visit tidy. Drop cloths go down before tools come out, and the work area is cleaned up before the plumber leaves.

Licensing and permits

Illinois licenses plumbers through the Department of Public Health and sets the plumbing standard in the Illinois Plumbing Code, and the village of Hanover Park registers plumbing contractors before it issues permits. You can check any plumber's license on the state's online lookup tool before you hire.

Where the village requires a permit, such as for a water heater replacement or a sewer repair, we pull it and schedule the inspection.
