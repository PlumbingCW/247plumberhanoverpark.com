Plumbing emergency

What to do while you wait for a plumber

The first few minutes decide how much damage a leak or backup causes. These steps are safe for any homeowner and work in any house in town.

Shut off the water

Turn the main valve clockwise, usually near the water meter in the basement or utility room. For one fixture, use the small valve under the sink or behind the toilet.

Cut power near water

If water is near outlets, appliances or the panel, switch off those breakers only if the floor around the panel is dry. Otherwise stay out and call us.

Call and describe it

Tell the dispatcher where the water is coming from, what you have already shut off, and whether any water is reaching electrical equipment.

Protect what you can

Move rugs, furniture and electronics away from the wet area. Photograph the damage for your insurance claim before cleanup.

Local knowledge

Plumbing in a village built in the 1960s and 1970s

Hanover Park was incorporated in 1958, and the Greenbrook subdivision came out of that first building boom. Woodlake Trails and Olde Salem followed in the early 1970s, so many homes here have plumbing that is now 50 years old or close to it. That age shows up as corroded supply lines, slow cast iron or clay sewer drains, and water heaters that have been replaced once or twice already.

The village gets all of its water from Lake Michigan through the Northwest Suburban Municipal Joint Action Water Agency. Reports for the same Chicago supply describe moderately hard water, around 8 grains per gallon in Streamwood's 2023 report. In practice that means scale builds up inside water heaters and on fixtures, which is why flushing and anode checks matter here.

Flood-prone streets

County hazard planning lists the Barrington and Irving Park Road area, the Highlands subdivision and Schick Road as flood-prone. Basements there need working sump pumps and clear floor drains.

Permits and inspections

The village Inspectional Services Division registers plumbing contractors and issues permits. Work such as a water heater replacement or sewer repair follows the Illinois Plumbing Code, the state standard for plumbing work.

Old sewer pipe

Clay and cast iron building sewers from the 1960s and 70s crack and collect roots. A camera inspection shows the real condition before anyone digs.

Pricing

What affects the cost of a plumbing job

Every job is different, so we price after we see it. These are the factors that move a quote up or down, in plain terms.

Access

A pipe in an open basement ceiling is quick to reach. One behind a finished wall or under a slab takes more time and more repair work.

Parts and materials

Pipe type, fixture grade, water heater size and fuel type all change the material cost.

Time and urgency

Simple clears are short. Complex repairs, permit work and after-hours emergencies take more labor.

Code and permits

Some work needs a village permit and inspection, which adds steps but protects your home and insurance.

Insurance note: homeowners policies often cover sudden water damage from a burst pipe, but not slow leaks or wear. Ask your insurer before cleanup, and keep photos and receipts.
